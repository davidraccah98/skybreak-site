Training

Before a game, pick 3 challenges: share of team gold, kill participation, vision lead over your lane opponent, damage taken per death, and more, each at an easy, medium or hard level.

Every challenge compares you with the average player of your own tier, champion and role. Rank says where you are. Skybreak shows how far above your level you play, and whether you keep doing it game after game.

Contests

Compete on your schedule

No brackets, no waiting for organizers, no team to schedule. Join a contest, play your normal solo queue games whenever you want, and climb the contest leaderboard.

Because everyone is measured against their own tier, a Bronze and a Challenger player compete on equal terms. Only your own performance counts.

How a game works

Lock in, then just play

  1. Pick 3 challenges before the game starts.
  2. Play. No overlay, no pop-ups, nothing on screen. Just your game.
  3. Get scored automatically when the game ends, against the average player of your tier, champion and role.
  4. See your edge grow in training, or your place on the leaderboard in a contest.

Why we built it

From a player, for players

I have played League since I was 15. I climbed to Diamond 2, in promos back when promos existed, playing at 25 fps on a computer that wasn't made for the game and without the money for a coach. I played in semi-professional teams in Italy and sat through their reviews. Then study and work took that time away. I kept playing, and today I'm way worse.

Back then a tournament was never just five games. It was hours of waiting for organizers, results and leaderboards, and very little actual gameplay. Most players are where I am today: they love the game and want to improve and compete, but they can't organize their life around it.

Skybreak is what I wanted back then, and still want today.

— the founder
