Same player, two goals

Two ways to play above your level

Rank says where you are. Skybreak shows how far above your level you play, and lets you compete on it.

Free

Training

Before a game, pick 3 challenges, each at an easy, medium or hard level. Every challenge compares you with the average player of your own tier, champion and role.

  • 14 challenges: farm, gold, kills, vision, lane, survival, objectives
  • See how often you beat your tier, game after game
  • Free, as many games as you like
Contests

Compete on your schedule

No brackets, no waiting for organizers, no team to schedule. Join a contest, play your normal solo queue game whenever you want, and climb the leaderboard.

  • 20 players per contest, from any tier
  • Everyone is measured against their own tier: Bronze and Challenger on equal terms
  • Free contests, and paid ones where 80% of the tickets go back as prizes

Lock in, then just play

How a game works

  1. Lock your pickChampion, role and 3 challenges, before the game starts. After that, nothing changes.
  2. PlayNo overlay, no pop-ups, nothing on screen. Just your game.
  3. Get scored automaticallySkybreak finds your game through Riot's official API and scores it against your tier, champion and role.
  4. See where you standYour edge in training, or your place on the contest leaderboard.

From a player, for players

Why we built it

I have played League since I was 15 and climbed to Diamond 2 on a computer that ran it at 25 fps. Today I study and work, and I'm way worse. Most players are where I am: they love the game and want to improve and compete, but can't organize their life around it. Skybreak is what I wanted back then, and still want today. — the founder
